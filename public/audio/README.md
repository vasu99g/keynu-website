# Background audio

Place the ambient instrumental loop here as **`ambient.mp3`**.

## What to source

- Format: MP3 (128–192 kbps) or AAC
- Duration: 30–90 seconds (loops seamlessly)
- Mood: soft Indian instrumental — sitar, tampura drone, bansuri, or sarod
- Volume: mastered low (the player attenuates further to ~18% by default)
- Licensing: royalty-free or fully licensed for commercial use (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, or commissioned)

## Recommended free sources

- [Pixabay Music — Indian instrumental](https://pixabay.com/music/search/indian/)
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/)
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://studio.youtube.com/) (filter by attribution-not-required, Indian/world)

When the file is in place no other change is required — the player picks it up automatically.
